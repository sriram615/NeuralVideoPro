## Analysis

### Strengths
- **100% on hard questions** — The system correctly handles inference and synthesis questions requiring full-video understanding.
- **Cross-domain generalization** — Questions span cooking, education, politics, sports, AI, space, personal vlog, and comedy; the system retrieves the correct video for all domains.
- **LLM-generated ground truth** — Questions were generated by an LLM from actual transcripts, not hand-curated, making this a more scalable and less biased evaluation method.

### Failures (2/42)
Both failures are on the **Trump press conference** video:
1. **Easy:** "Who is mentioned as being from Russia?" — Not retrieved in top-5.
2. **Medium:** "What does the speaker say should happen to end the war?" — Not retrieved in top-5.

**Likely cause:** The Trump video contains dense, fast-paced, overlapping speech with frequent interruptions. Whisper transcripts are fragmented, and CLIP visual embeddings from a press conference setting may not align well with specific factual queries about "Russia" or "ending the war."
